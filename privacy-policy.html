Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Wasta ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and what rights you have over it. We've written it to apply consistently to everyone who uses the app, wherever you are in the world, using the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as our baseline standard since that's the strictest framework we're subject to as an operator based in Germany.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Wasta is currently operated by an individual, not a registered company. See our Legal Notice for full identifying and contact details. For the purposes of data protection law, this person is the "controller" of your data.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password (password is never visible to us — it's handled directly by our authentication provider, Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile data: your name, a short bio, a profile photo (optional), and any entries you choose to add under profile categories (e.g. places you've lived or visited, skills, languages, sports, hobbies, profession). You control what you add here.
  • Phone number: optional, used only to power the WhatsApp contact button shown to your accepted contacts.
  • Contacts data: who you've added as a contact, and requests you've sent or received.
  • Usage data: basic technical data needed to operate the app, such as device type and app version, collected through our backend provider (Firebase/Google Cloud).

We do not use advertising or analytics tracking in this app, and we don't sell your data to anyone, ever.

4. Who we share data with

We use Firebase (a Google Cloud product) as our backend infrastructure provider for authentication and data storage. Google acts as our data processor under a data processing agreement and standard contractual clauses for any data transferred outside the EU/EEA. We do not share your data with any other third party, and we do not sell data to advertisers or data brokers.

Other users of the app can see:

  • Your public profile details (name, photo, bio, profile categories), once you've chosen to add them.
  • Your contact details (email, phone), only once you've mutually accepted a contact connection with them.
  • A numeric count of how many connections you have, visible to any signed-in user (never the list of who those connections are).

5. International data transfers

Because you've told us you intend to invite users from around the world: our infrastructure (Firebase/Google Cloud) may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where this involves transferring data out of the EU/EEA, we rely on Google's Standard Contractual Clauses as the transfer safeguard required under GDPR. By using the app, you acknowledge that your data may be processed in a country with different data protection laws than your own.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and profile data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account), we permanently delete your profile, your contacts and requests on both sides of every connection, your private contact information, and your notifications. This action cannot be undone.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (editable directly in your Profile).
  • Delete your data (available directly in-app at any time, via Settings → Delete Account).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for anything based on consent (e.g. by removing an item from your profile).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in our Legal Notice. If you're in the EU/EEA, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

8. Children

Wasta is not intended for anyone under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Security

We use Firebase's security infrastructure and our own access-control rules to restrict who can read or write each piece of data — for example, your private contact details are only ever readable by you and your accepted contacts, never by the public. No system is completely secure, but we've deliberately scoped access as narrowly as possible throughout the app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the contact address in our Legal Notice.
