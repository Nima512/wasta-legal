Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what personal data Wasta ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and what rights you have over it. We've written it to apply consistently to everyone who uses the app, wherever you are in the world, using the EU's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) as our baseline standard since that's the strictest framework we're subject to as an operator based in Germany.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Wasta is currently operated by an individual, not a registered company. See our Legal Notice for full identifying and contact details. For the purposes of data protection law, this person is the "controller" of your data.

2. What data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and password (password is never visible to us — it's handled directly by our authentication provider, Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile data: your name, a short bio, a profile photo (optional), and any entries you choose to add under profile categories (e.g. places you've lived or visited, skills, languages, sports, hobbies, profession). You control what you add here.
  • Phone number: optional, used only to power the WhatsApp contact button shown to your accepted contacts. Tapping that button opens WhatsApp directly on your device; from that point, your conversation is governed by Meta's own privacy policy, not ours.
  • Contacts data: who you've added as a contact, and requests you've sent or received.
  • Report data: if you report another user, we store who filed the report, who it's about, the reason you selected, and any additional details you provide, so we can review and act on it.
  • Feedback data: if you use Send Feedback in Settings, we store the text you submit together with your account ID, so we can read and act on it.
  • Usage data: basic technical data needed to operate the app (such as device type and app version), collected only through the core Firebase Authentication and Firestore SDKs that power the app's functionality. We do not integrate Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, Crashlytics, or any advertising SDK — Analytics and Ads are explicitly disabled in our configuration.

Because the app is distributed through Apple TestFlight, Apple also separately collects data needed to operate TestFlight itself — such as your email address (to send the install invitation) and crash/diagnostic data from your device, if you opt in to sharing it with developers. Apple acts as an independent data controller for this data under its own privacy policy, not on our behalf; see Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.

You control second-degree visibility directly: an entry is only matchable by people outside your contacts while it's present on your profile. Removing it (Profile → edit → delete the entry) immediately withdraws that consent — from then on it's visible only to your direct contacts, the same way adding it is what grants that consent in the first place.

4. Who we share data with

We use Firebase (a Google Cloud product) as our backend infrastructure provider for authentication and data storage. Google acts as our data processor under a data processing agreement and standard contractual clauses for any data transferred outside the EU/EEA. We do not share your data with any other third party, and we do not sell data to advertisers or data brokers.

Other users of the app can see:

  • Your public profile details (name, photo, bio, profile categories), once you've chosen to add them.
  • Your contact details (email, phone), only once you've mutually accepted a contact connection with them.
  • A numeric count of how many connections you have, visible to any signed-in user (never the list of who those connections are).

This Privacy Policy and our other legal documents are hosted on GitHub Pages. Simply visiting that website (separate from using the app itself) causes GitHub to log your IP address and basic request data, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. We don't control this logging and it's unrelated to your use of the app itself.

5. International data transfers

Our primary database (Firestore) is hosted in the eur3 region (a multi-region location spanning Belgium and the Netherlands), meaning your core app data is stored within the EU. Some processing by our infrastructure provider, Google (which operates Firebase), may still involve the United States — for example, authentication and certain operational services. Where that happens, Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF), which we rely on as our primary legal basis for these transfers, with Google's Standard Contractual Clauses as a backup safeguard. By using the app, you acknowledge that some processing may occur outside your own country under these protections.

6. How long we keep your data

We keep your account and profile data for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account), we permanently delete your profile, your contacts and requests on both sides of every connection, your private contact information, and your notifications. This action cannot be undone.

If you report another user, we keep that report for up to 12 months after it's resolved, in case we need to revisit an enforcement decision, and then delete it.

If you submit feedback, we keep it for up to 12 months to inform app improvements, and then delete it.

We do not currently maintain backups of the database. When you delete data, it is removed from our systems immediately.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate data (editable directly in your Profile).
  • Delete your data (available directly in-app at any time, via Settings → Delete Account).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for anything based on consent (e.g. by removing an item from your profile).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in our Legal Notice. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As the controller is based in Hesse, Germany, the competent authority there is the Hessischer Beauftragter für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit (HBDI) — but if you live in the EU/EEA, you can also complain to your own local authority instead.

8. Children

Wasta is not intended for anyone under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under this age, and account creation requires confirming you meet it. If you believe an underage account has been created, please contact us so we can remove it.

9. Security

We use Firebase's security infrastructure and our own access-control rules to restrict who can read or write each piece of data — for example, your private contact details are only ever readable by you and your accepted contacts, never by the public. No system is completely secure, but we've deliberately scoped access as narrowly as possible throughout the app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update minor or non-material parts of this policy as the app evolves, and will update the "Last updated" date when we do. For any material change — one that meaningfully affects how we process your data — we will notify you inside the app and ask you to actively accept the updated policy before you can continue using Wasta. Simply continuing to use the app is not treated as acceptance of a material change.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the contact address in our Legal Notice.
